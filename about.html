Who We Are

Bokori Technical and Vocational Training College (BTVTC) was established in 2025 to provide high-quality technical and vocational education aimed at empowering Kenyan youth with practical skills for self-reliance, innovation, and employment.

Our Mission

To provide quality training and produce competent graduates who contribute positively to Kenya's socio-economic development.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution in providing technical, entrepreneurial, and innovative education in Kenya.
